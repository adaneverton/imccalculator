FAQ

Questions we always get

Do I need a finished 3D file?

No. If you have the file (STL, OBJ, 3MF or STEP) the process is faster. If you do not, we model the part from photos, drawings, measurements or the original piece.

How long does production take?

It depends on size, quantity and material. Small pieces are usually ready in a few days; larger batches and projects that need modelling take longer. The exact date comes with the quote, before anything is printed.

How is the price calculated?

We look at material volume, printing time, the finishing required and, when needed, the modelling hours. Larger quantities bring the unit price down.

Is there a minimum order?

Not for catalogue products — we print from a single unit. For personalised corporate gifts we work from 20 pieces, so the design cost is worth it.

Will the part survive sun, heat and hard use?

Yes, as long as the material fits. PLA is not suited to a car interior or outdoor use, but PETG, ASA and fibre-filled compounds handle those conditions well. Tell us where the part will live and we will point to the best option.

Do you print copyrighted designs?

We only reproduce models that are licensed for use or authorised by the author. For trademarked characters and brands, we create original alternatives or work with files cleared for commercial use.

How does shipping work?

We deliver anywhere in Ireland with An Post or a courier, with a tracking number. Around Dublin you can also arrange collection or local delivery.
